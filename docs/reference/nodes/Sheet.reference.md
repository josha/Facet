### Sheet

<!-- aliases: Sheet | sheet -->

Open one option group below. Each group gives part of this contract.

A bottom sheet's plate continues past the safe area to the edge of the glass,
and into the side cutouts when the sheet spans the screen. Its content, grabber
and actions stay in the safe area. Side and centered sheets stop at the safe
area. Keep `CoreUISafeInsets` on the app.
