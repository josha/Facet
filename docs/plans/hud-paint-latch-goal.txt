WHERE THE DEFECT MUST THEREFORE LIVE

Everything model-side is exonerated by direct measurement. What remains is the
ADAPTER AND RENDERER — src/client/screen_target.luau, src/render/renderer.luau,
src/present/presenter.luau — and specifically whatever a REAL orientation change
on real hardware does that a scripted viewport resize in Studio does not.

Leads, offered as leads and not conclusions. Measure before choosing:
  • A surface root's visibility is ScreenGui.Enabled (screen_target.luau:1752) —
    a different property on a class none of the node paths resolve to. A node
    can report Visible = true inside a screen that is off.
  • Instance RECYCLING is on by default. A pooled instance that is reused or not
    restored across a dismissal + re-solve is the shape that would survive every
    model-side check ever written, and the sink dismissal is the step that makes
    this reproduction different from every failed one.
  • Presentation shift: screenRectOf accumulates presentationShift(path), so a
    shift left non-zero after dismissal survives a re-solve.
  • presenter.dismiss's teardown — the displayLayer reset when the stack
    empties, retireSurface(), retireRevealFor(handle), catcher disposal.
  • Elision: the round's baseline is 43 %-elided, and an elided container that
    fails to re-materialise paints nothing while reporting a legal solve.

YOUR INSTRUMENT — it is honest now, and it was not before

examples/gallery/scenarios/hud.luau carries a PAINT PROBE on its own presenter
surface (separate mount, controller, solve and visibility walk, so the walk that
stops cannot take the readout with it). Commit 748df2f made it truthful; before
that it printed "13 of 13 painting" over a broken screen, which is what a HEALTHY
plate prints at that viewport. Read artifacts/navigation-and-menus/h4a-paint-probe.md.

It now reports three states and cannot collapse them:
    healthy      11 of 14 rows wanted / skipped: … / 11 of 11 painting
    lost          1 of 14 NOT PAINTED / <names> / hidden under: <node>
    unmeasured   ENGINE SIDE UNAVAILABLE — MODEL ONLY
It compares the model's opinion against the engine's for fourteen rows, latches
on the frame they first disagree, and runs a second 4 Hz clock so a HUD that
stops solving cannot freeze it on a stale reading. It is proved to fire: hiding
one round disc gives `1 of 14 NOT PAINTED / Rounds / hidden under: …/R3`.

`hidden under:` is the line that matters. Seven rows lost under ONE node is a
different defect from seven lost separately.
