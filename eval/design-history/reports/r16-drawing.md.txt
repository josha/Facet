# Ink Orbit

Ink Orbit is a local drawing challenge for mouse, touch, and controller. Pick one of three missions, draw on a blank sheet, and finish the actual drawing as a print. Edit returns to the same sheet and keeps the tool, ink, brush, history, and canvas view. Starting over asks before replacing the sheet.

Open `InkOrbit.rbxlx` in Studio and press Play. The four Luau files and `default.project.json` are the source for the playable Rojo build. Studio's final Xbox Play session starts on the mission chooser. The native-controller route is D-pad and A for UI, right stick for the on-paper cursor, RT to draw, and LB with left stick to select a tool on release. B cancels the chooser. Touch can tap the equipped-tool tile and a choice, or hold the tile and drag. Mouse uses the same tile and supports wheel zoom and right-drag pan.

I did not inspect ZoomView before choosing the initial custom camera. I inspected its contract during refinement. Its capture and camera lock integrate with child drags, while this app owns a separate draw gesture and cursor and uses LB for the tool ring. The existing explicit zoom and pan mode avoids competing for those inputs.

It cannot use these local objects as registered tool icons. The wheel keeps complete, readable text labels, and the equipped-tool key shows the matching original art beside its text.
