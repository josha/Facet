## Conclusion

The guidance produced two recognizable, original directions: Postmark's airmail drawing desk and Night Heron's Art Deco blackjack table. It has not demonstrated dependable finished UI on the first request. Both builds needed substantial internal repair; Postmark also needed explicit user feedback and parent-discovered repairs. Good artwork, a matching palette and correct control names did not prevent clipping, weak selected states, detached controls or failed native controller actions. The evaluation is complete with the limitations below; the builds do not earn an unrestricted all-input release pass.

These are two development genres already used during guidance work, not an unseen benchmark or a general success rate. A first complete delivery may include the frozen workflow's own reviews, but later user and parent intervention must not be credited to an unaided result.

## Design findings

Postmark's player's drawing leads on warm stock. Its original courier, lettering, postage tab and striped paper edge give it character. The controls and radial treatment are less distinctive than the illustration; this is a stronger identity than a recolored utility screen, but the craft was uneven. A ring was explicitly required by the evaluation brief, so this trial cannot establish that a radial is the best brush chooser. The custom opener originally omitted its anchor, which explains the centered popup. The repair follows the opener and allows safe inward fitting. The label-only Ink submenu initially used a fitted list in short landscape; the later swatch version fits as a ring at all four tested sizes. Earlier list screenshots are preserved as earlier evidence, not presented as the final state.

Night Heron has the more integrated finished composition. Cards, chip rings, felt, brass actions and restrained type belong together. The result now reads clearly while Next hand stays in its established action area. Selected stake and navigational focus have separate visible treatments. Desktop secondary text remains modest, and the Rules focus curl has a close optical approach to the final paragraph. These are craft reservations, not evidence that the main task is obstructed.

## Tidekeepers clipping

The reported cut left and bottom edges were a shared Dialog layout defect. Insets sat outside a clipping boundary, so button borders and shadows met the boundary and were cut. The repair moves the spacing inside the clipped content and clears inherited shell padding that otherwise narrows the inner area. It also reserves painted clearance when scrolling a focused action into view.

Desktop and both phone orientations show complete button outlines and shadows, with 24 px left clearance and 19 px below the actions. Actual pointer/Touch open, cancel, accept and return routes passed. Live controller focus was not established for this separate place; an engine-double regression checks focused paint clearance. The original saved Studio and original checkpoint were preserved. See the [repair evidence](../forward-r22-ab/build-03/directed-clipping-repair/README.md).

## Implications for first-pass guidance

1. Require an explicit intended experience and a complete initial composition. Judge subject, choices, action and feedback together. A strong hero illustration does not establish the rest of the interface.
2. Separate a control-contract exercise from a design-choice evaluation. Requiring RadialMenu answers whether the agent can use it; it cannot answer whether a nearby popup would serve the task better. If a radial is chosen, its anchor, inward fit and list fallback are part of the interaction design.
3. Make painted bounds a library invariant. Focus rings, shadows and styled borders need clearance inside clipping surfaces. This belongs in shared layout and regression coverage, rather than repeated instructions to individual game authors to add padding.
4. Inspect the first real screen with meaningful selected/focused states, long live labels and both phone orientations. The Size and Marker regressions show why default labels and element rectangles are insufficient.
5. Exercise complete native input routes early. Actual trigger/stick drawing and controller Back exposed failures that static checks and assigned focus missed. Keep state through rotation and return, and record when the simulator changes input mode.
6. Calibrate critique to the owner's quality bar. Independent scoring helped find defects, but cannot prove delight or substitute for the parent's visual judgment. Preserve quiet conventional interfaces where they fit the product; do not turn these findings into a universal demand for more art or ornament.

No guidance change was made during this evaluation. The report and final captures must be frozen before a subsequent guidance revision.
