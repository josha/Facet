# Moth & Mortar

Moth & Mortar is a local, single-player demo of a traveling apothecary on the Lantern Road. The shelf has six illustrated goods, and the selected item appears in a botanical alcove beside its inspection and purchase docket. The session starts with 160 coins.

- The Rojo mapping includes the persistent client script and its companion artwork folder. [Client script](FantasyShop.client.luau) · [Packed artwork](FantasyShopArt)
- Open the local build in Roblox Studio and press Play. The live lease place kept its existing Facet package, floor, spawn and archives. Its prior VelvetJack LocalScript was disabled without inspecting its source. [MothAndMortar.rbxlx](evidence/MothAndMortar.rbxlx)

## Review and checks

- Round 1 reviewed the rendered design target before the static Studio art proof or purchase implementation. [Independent verdict](evidence/r1/verdict.json).
- Round 2 reviewed actual captures from all four forms. [Packet](evidence/r2/PACKET.md) and [independent verdict](evidence/r2/verdict.json).
- Round 3 passed desktop, portrait and landscape. Xbox met the visual scores and called for an explicit route from shelf focus to Buy. The final guide now names D-pad Right from the shelf and D-pad Left from Buy. Both prompts were checked in Studio through programmatic focus. The three-round limit ended independent scoring before this focused repair. [Round 3 verdict](evidence/r3/verdict.json) · [Post-review Xbox probe](evidence/xbox-focus-probe.json)
- All three review agents were spawned with `fork_turns="none"` and no model override. They inherited the author's GPT‑6.1 Sol configuration. Each received the critic brief and its packet path.
- Studio exposed a 200,000-character Source limit and required an explicit Zstd frame size. The local art was split into three focused modules and packed with known frame lengths.
- `lute run tools/facet verify FantasyShop.client.luau --static` passed strict types, usage lint and the Rojo build. Lint flags explicit paint and fonts used for the custom art, plus the engine TV distance fallback that keeps a large layout after pointer input. [Static evidence](evidence/static-verify.txt).
- Facet's live design review ran on each form. Its hollow-plate heuristic missed the editable texture on the item display. The joined-facts heuristic flagged the unit-price purchase caption and a console control guide. The purchase caption keeps the quantity and cost together beside separate stock and ownership values. [Runtime review evidence](evidence/r3) · [Post-review Xbox rules](evidence/xbox-post-r3-design.txt).
- The live session used the Studio Device Simulator. Its console output includes an asset-type warning for `rbxassetid://203785492` also seen before this storefront mounted. The local shop artwork uses editable textures. The output also records two `Setting GuiService.SelectedObject to invalid GuiObject` warnings. The fresh session nevertheless reported `InspectTonic` as the active selection and the capture shows its outline. [Output](evidence/xbox-freeze-output.txt)
