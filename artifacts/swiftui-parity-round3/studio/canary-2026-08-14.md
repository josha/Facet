## The two open questions this answered

### ITEM A — `flow_wrap` has now been mounted in an engine

The gate note's words: *"the `flow_wrap` fixture has never been mounted in Studio
under Play (the engine's cross-axis rule came from a live probe on 2026-08-13;
the fixture did not)."* It is mounted now, and the fixture's own A/B — the whole
argument for `wrap` being a prop — was driven live at the phone profile
(389×762):

| | wrap ON | wrap OFF |
|---|---|---|
| lines the fourteen tags occupy | **4** (`y` = 182, 234, 286, 338) | 1 (`y` = 182) |
| right edge of the last tag | 324 (inside the 389 viewport) | **1183** |
| `controller.diagnostics()` | **empty** | `content overflows this hstack by 806px on the main axis` |
| the fixture's own status line | `Fitting: the solver reports nothing.` | `Overflowing: the solver is reporting it, live.` |

Captures: [`a-flow-wrap-on-389.png`](a-flow-wrap-on-389.png),
[`a-flow-wrap-off-389.png`](a-flow-wrap-off-389.png).

**And the pictures say something the numbers do not.** With `wrap` off the solver
reports 806px of overflow and the instances really are laid out to x=1183 — but
only five tags are *painted*. The page's own `UI.ScrollView` (`/FlowWrap/Page`,
`ClipsDescendants = true`) crops the rest. So on this surface the diagnostic's
"it will paint outside its box" is the honest worst case rather than what a
player sees; the engine's clip host absorbs it. Which is exactly the contrast the
next item is about.

*(One authoring note, not a defect: `steps.setWrap` turns wrapping off on the
payload `"off"` and treats every other payload — including `"false"` — as ON.
A first drive with `"false"` silently measured the wrapped state twice.)*

### ITEM D2 — the engine does **not** clip a `Path2D` stroke, and `UpdateControlPoint` works when parented

Two separate findings, both previously unanswered.

**(a) `ClipsDescendants` does not crop a `Path2D`.** A scope-owned probe drew the
same octagon in two 200×200 panels, one with `ClipsDescendants = true` and one
without, centred on the panel's right edge so half the ring must fall outside.
`GetBoundingRect` returns 147×147 for **both** (confirming the note's point that
it knows nothing about a host's crop) — and the pixels show the stroke continuing
past the clipping panel's edge, identically to the non-clipping one:

![Path2D is not clipped](d2-path2d-not-clipped.png)

Ordinary GuiObjects *are* clipped by the same property (item A's unwrapped row,
above, is proof in the same session). **A circular progress ring placed inside a
scrolling or clipping host will therefore paint outside it**, and no diagnostic
sees it, because the solver's geometry and `GetBoundingRect` both agree it is
in-box. That is a real, live-only defect class for `presentation = "circular"`.

**(b) `Path2D:UpdateControlPoint` — the cheap in-place mitigation — is available
in the shipped configuration.** Probed as a pair in one session:

| | result |
|---|---|
| **parented** `Path2D` (the framework's own, under `/ProgressRing/Page/Ring/Ring/Arc`) | **succeeds** |
| unparented `Path2D` (created loose, `SetControlPoints` accepted) | refused — `Attempting to use Path2D with invalid parent` |

The gate note recorded the refusal and said it "was never re-probed under Play".
The refusal is a *parenting* rule, not a capability gap, and LuauUI always parents
its `Path2D` under a holder Frame — so the in-place update path is open.

**(c) the arc's per-frame cost, in writes.** `progress_ring` at the phone profile
with the determinate ring and both indeterminate arcs live: `controller.stats()`
`propWrites` went **121 → 9,169 across 120 heartbeats — ~75 property writes per
frame**. `controller.refresh()` itself is not where that lands (mean 0.0071 ms,
worst 0.0091 ms over the same 120 frames), because the arc rebuild rides the
motion clock's step, not the render pass. **This is a desktop Studio number and
is not a phone budget** — it is a magnitude, for the phone measurement to be
compared against.

## What the real adapter showed that the fake target cannot

Every scenario below mounted clean at both the phone (389×762) and desktop
(1280×720) profiles, with **`controller.diagnostics()` empty in all fourteen
mounts**.

| scenario | instances materialised | classes the adapter really created | clip hosts | text nodes / truncated / disclosed (phone) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `flow_wrap` | 141 | 43 `UICorner`, 41 `UIStroke`, 23 `TextButton`, 21 `UIPadding`, 6 `TextLabel`, 3 `Frame`, 2 `ImageLabel`, 1 `ScrollingFrame` | 1 | 27 / 0 / 0 |
| `progress_ring` | 30 | **3 `Path2D`**, 9 `Frame`, 6 `TextLabel`, 5 `UIStroke`, 5 `UICorner`, 1 `ScrollingFrame` | 1 | 6 / 0 / 0 |
| `hud` | 167 | 44 `UICorner`, 42 `UIStroke`, 32 `Frame`, 20 `TextLabel`, 15 `TextButton`, 9 `UIPadding`, 4 `ImageLabel` | 0 | 29 / **6** / 0 |
| `text_degrade` | 39 | 9 `TextLabel`, 7 `UIStroke`, 7 `UICorner`, 5 `TextButton`, 5 `UIPadding`, 4 `Frame`, 1 `ScrollingFrame` | 1 | 14 / 1 / **2** |
| `variable_extents` | 58 | 12 `TextButton`, 11 `TextLabel`, 11 `UICorner`, 10 `UIStroke`, 8 `UIPadding`, 2 `ImageLabel` | 1 | 19 / 0 / 0 |
| `row_capabilities` | 81 | 22 `Frame`, 22 `TextLabel`, 10 `UICorner`, 9 `TextButton`, 8 `UIStroke`, 8 `UIPadding` | 1 | 30 / 0 / **12** |
| `lifecycle_hidden` | 40 | 11 `TextLabel`, 9 `UICorner`, 6 `UIStroke`, 4 `Frame`, 4 `TextButton`, 2 `ImageLabel` | 1 | 13 / 0 / 0 |

Three things in that table only a real engine can say:

1. **The text cascade is running against real bounds.** `text_degrade` at 389
   wide reports `/TextDegrade/Page/Lead` measured by the engine at
   `TextBounds = 319.5×24` in a 326×29 box at size 12 — *two* lines — and the
   same node at 1280 wide measures `464.5×12` in a 475×15 box, one line. The
   node re-measured and the cascade re-decided; the disclose set
   (`WrapPanel/WrapBody`, `Title`) is the engine's answer, not a synthetic one.
   `hud` reports six truncated labels at the phone profile with `policy = "truncate"`.
2. **Elision is real, and elided candidates materialise at zero size.** `hud`
   elides 26 nodes of 75 created; the losing `ViewThatFits` candidate
   `…/StripFit/ObjectiveShort/ObjectiveShortT` is a live `TextLabel` with
   `AbsoluteSize = 0×0` and `TextFits = false`. A fake target has no `TextFits`.
3. **The framework materialises invisible `GuiButton`s on purpose.** Every
   `hud` node lying outside the viewport box is a `LuauUIHitExpander` —
   `TextButton`, `Visible = true`, `BackgroundTransparency = 1`, reaching 8px
   below a 762-high viewport and 5px left of x=0. Nothing *paints* there. This
   is the shipped shape behind **item D**, and it is the reason the framework
   holds the invisible-button rule itself rather than leaning on the engine.

## What could NOT be checked, and why

**The Play bridge is unavailable in this environment — this is the blocking
finding, not an excuse.** `mcp__Roblox_Studio__start_stop_play` times out on
every call and then answers `Start play hasn't finished yet` for the remainder of
that Studio instance's life, and while a session *is* in Play,
`execute_luau` answers `Target is not reachable (createExecuteLuauBridge_loadCodeAsync, …)`
for **both** the Client and the Server datamodel. Reproduced on **two separate
Studio instances** (`LuauUI-Showcase` and `LuauUI-PerformanceLab`), the second
one fresh, so it is not one wedged session. Synthetic input cannot substitute:
`CGEvent` mouse clicks and `System Events` keystrokes/menu selections were all
posted to Studio and **none** reached it (proved by clicking an Explorer row and
a ribbon tab with no selection change).

So these stay open:
