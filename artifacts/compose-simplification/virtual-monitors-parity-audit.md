Three additional integration risks need live evidence:

- **Chat overlap/readability:** main capped the conversation at 960px and adapted its welcome for short heights. Current [Suggestions](../../examples/virtual_monitors/screens.luau#L1339) has an unbounded natural height at `Y=75`, while the composer is fixed at `height−78`. Test empty Chat at 844×390 and 320px width for overlap; inspect long replies at 1920px width.
- **Scroll preservation during mode transitions:** [collection()](../../examples/virtual_monitors/screens.luau#L163) lets both presentations publish `CanvasPosition` into one shared cell. Main restricted publication to the active presentation. Switch modes near the bottom of Games/Chat, where differing viewport heights cause clamping; verify no scroll jump or competing updates.
- **Touch drag versus scrolling:** [SaveDrag](../../examples/virtual_monitors/screens.luau#L395) installs an immediately enabled native detector without the old hold-to-arm arbitration. Flick the catalog, then hold and drag onto Saved; both interactions must work independently.

The procedural/R15 scene implementation and catalog/chat data behavior remain essentially unchanged. Native theme color transitions are still implemented through `StyleRule:SetPropertyTransitions`; their deletion should **not** be claimed. However, fixed typography, missing compact HUD values, changed hero geometry and removal of the television default-to-Screen rule still require side-by-side live checks in both themes.
