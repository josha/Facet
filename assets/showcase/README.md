# Showcase artwork

Original G-rated illustrations generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool
for this showcase on 2026-09-29. The PNG files are unmodified generator outputs.
Uploaded as Roblox Image assets using the authorized workspace Open Cloud key;
no credentials are stored here. `upload-manifest.json` records asset IDs, image
sizes, upload operation IDs and the last observed moderation state.

| File | Design brief | Use |
|---|---|---|
| sipworks-teas.png | Fifteen gouache teacups and botanicals, three columns by five rows, warm varied colors, no text | Recipe cards and detail |
| glade-garden.png | Gentle storybook woodland, pond at lower left and nectar feeder at lower right, no characters | Interactive garden |
| glade-wisps.png | Three friendly amber, lavender and yellow-green leaf creatures, transparent background | Discovered garden visitors |
| pack-equipment.png | Eight isolated cozy adventure items, four columns by two rows, transparent background, rounded toy sword and climbing pick, no violence | Inventory cards |
| cartwheel-market.png | Wood and teal potion cart, colorful bottles and village market, no people or text | Order board banner |

The tea and wisp atlases are cropped with native ImageRectOffset/ImageRectSize.
Their coordinates refer to Roblox's downscaled textures (maximum dimension
1024), rather than the original PNG dimensions. Crops were checked in Studio.

The prompts requested family-friendly original artwork without text, existing
characters or violence. Recipe illustrations are decorative; ingredient names
and quantities come from Sipworks' catalog.
